01 · concept

a — c
a — origin

When the network drops, when the data plan is empty, when the app is broken — a shortcode still works. Dial *123# and you reach what you need. It's the most reliable line to a solution we have.

b — thesis

shortkohdz exists to be that line, scaled. A holding company whose ventures are direct paths to real solutions for real pain points — access first, friction last.

c — symbol

The asterisk is the syntax. We took it back from the keyboard and drew it as the master mark — six radii, one hot point. Each arm is a venture. The center is where you dial in.

02 · the mark — anatomy

geometry
01

Six tapered radii

Six arms — wider at the root, narrower at the tip — set on 60° increments. Six because it's the count of current ventures (Bukr, LuxeBeau, SpeedPlus, Cedar, Sozo, +1) and a balanced figure that scales narratively as the holdco grows.

02

The dial-in core

A single coral dot at the centre — the only break in the monochrome. It's the literal "you are here" of a USSD session, and the only place the brand colour shows in the mark itself.

03

Inner safe ring

A 18-unit clear zone around the core keeps the dot legible at favicon scale and prevents the arms from collapsing into a solid star. The mark holds at 16px.

04

Drawn, not typed

This is not the typographic asterisk. The taper, the spacing, the proportion of arm-to-core are tuned — so the mark reads as a deliberate symbol, not a punctuation glyph.
